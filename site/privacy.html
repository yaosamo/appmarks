Bubbleg

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Bubbleg collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What the app reads, and why

  • Accessibility lets Bubbleg list your open windows, read their titles and positions, and bring the one you click to the front.
  • Screen Recording lets Bubbleg draw a small live preview of each window.

Previews and window titles are held in memory only while Bubbleg runs. They are never saved to disk, never sent anywhere and never shared with anyone, including the developer.

What stays on your Mac

Your settings (bubble size, position, appearance) are stored in the app's own preferences. A small log file records whether the permissions are granted and any errors, with no window titles or content. Deleting the app removes both.

This website

bubbleg.yaosamo.com uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It sets no cookies and does not identify you.

Children

Bubbleg collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome on the support page.
