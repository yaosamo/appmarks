Support

Something not working, or an idea? Open an issue on GitHub, or message @yaosamo. Every report is read.

No bubbles appear

Bubbleg needs Accessibility to see your windows. Click the Bubbleg icon in the menu bar → Permissions… and turn Bubbleg on in the list System Settings opens.

The bubbles show icons, not previews

Previews need Screen Recording. Turn it on from Permissions…; macOS then asks to quit and reopen Bubbleg.

Open at login

Menu bar icon → Settings… → Open at login. It is off until you turn it on.

Moving the bubbles

Drag the panel by its background. It clicks onto screen edges and corners, and onto any side of the active window, where it follows whichever window you switch to.

Privacy

Bubbleg collects nothing. See the privacy policy.
